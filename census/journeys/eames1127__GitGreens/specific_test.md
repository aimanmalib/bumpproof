# Change the season and the theme

## The garden page loads
Verify the heading "GitGreens" is visible with a "Grow Garden" button and four season buttons: Spring, Summer, Autumn and Winter.

## Pick a season
Click the "Winter" season button. Verify the Winter button is now highlighted with a white background and the other season buttons are not.

## Switch the theme
Click the button that ends with "Garden" under the heading. Verify its label changes between "Dark Garden" and "Light Garden".

## The grow button needs a name
Verify the "Grow Garden" button is disabled while the username field is empty.
