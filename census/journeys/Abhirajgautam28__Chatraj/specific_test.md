# Move from the login form to the sign-up form

The app's API server is not part of the sandbox, so nothing is submitted.

## The login form loads
Verify the heading "Welcome Back" is visible with an email field and a password field.

## Open the sign-up form
Click the link to the register page below the form. Verify the address ends with "/register" and the heading "Create an Account" is shown.

## The sign-up form has its fields
Verify the sign-up form shows a field with the placeholder "John" and a field with the placeholder "Doe".
