# Change the scoreboard language

## Open the team menu
Click the team panel showing "Spieler 1 / Spieler 2". Verify a menu opens with the option "💥 Match (257)".

## Switch to German
Click "⚙️ Settings" (or "⚙️ Einstellungen"), then click "Deutsch". Verify the labels "Zielpunkte" and "Sprache" are visible.

## Switch to English
Click "English". Verify the labels "Target score" and "Language" are visible.
