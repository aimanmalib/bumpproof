# Enter demo mode and switch the language

## The login page loads
Verify the heading "Wyrmhort" is visible with the buttons "Sign in with Google" and "Try demo mode".

## Enter demo mode
Click "Try demo mode". Verify a banner that starts with "Demo-Modus aktiv" is shown.

## Switch to English
Click the button labelled "Switch to English". Verify the banner now starts with "Demo mode active".
